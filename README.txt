ONLY FOR YOU — বুশরার জন্য
==========================

এই ফোল্ডারেই জিহাদের দুইটা আসল ভয়েস রেকর্ডিং রেখে দাও:

  1. chumu-one.mp3   → "চুমু চাই" বাটনটি (Button 1) বাজাবে
  2. chumu-two.mp3   → "আরও চুমু চাই" বাটনটি (Button 2) বাজাবে

English instructions:
  Put the two prerecorded voice notes in THIS folder, next to this file:
    - chumu-one.mp3  (played by the "চুমু চাই" button)
    - chumu-two.mp3  (played by the "আরও চুমু চাই" button)
  Then redeploy the site (git push). The website auto-detects them.

Notes:
  • .mp3 is recommended. .m4a / .wav / .ogg / .aac also work.
  • Keep the exact names chumu-one and chumu-two (lowercase, hyphen).
  • The site also works without file names matching — open the "ভয়েস সংযোগ"
    panel on the page and pick the files from your device/browser instead
    (they are then saved inside that browser only).
  • No generated or AI voices are used. Only Jihad's own recordings.
